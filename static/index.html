About

Software, from embedded to cloud

Avid software developer with extensive experience designing software across the stack, beginning in 2015 — skilled in IoT, embedded development, .NET, Python, C++, Linux, and cloud architecture.

Founded my first company in 2016, building mobile applications for small businesses and local government. That led into IoT as Lead Developer at Lederbord, a startup building wireless, app-controlled LED scoreboards, where I designed multiple iterations of embedded firmware and native iOS/Android apps, and built out device management and OTA update infrastructure at scale.

After graduating from Colorado School of Mines, I continued in IoT at Chevron, building a .NET application for device management and the cloud infrastructure to process telemetry at scale. From there I moved into cloud architecture and DevOps — modernizing infrastructure to enterprise standards, improving security posture and performance, and leading a project to move a high-volume HPC simulation workload to object storage, cutting costs by 50%.
